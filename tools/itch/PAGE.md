## Uploads

1. `choirbreaker-demo.zip`: tick **This file will be played in the browser**. Leave "Paid" unticked: the browser demo is free to everyone.
2. `choirbreaker-full.zip`: set to **Windows / macOS / Linux** (it is a web page, so it runs on all three), and leave it as a paid download. Name it "Choirbreaker full game (all 36 sectors)".

## Embed options

- Viewport: 1280 x 720, tick **Mobile friendly** (landscape) and **Fullscreen button**.
- Tick **Automatically start on page load**: off (players click to start, so the sound can play).

## Description

> **Nine days after a red star sang to Earth, its swarm burned the planet in forty hours.**
>
> Forty thousand survivors are left on frozen outposts. From the wreck of one of the swarm's fighters they built the *Dissonance*: a human ship that wears the enemy's signature. You fly it into the system that sent them.
>
> **Play the first six sectors free, right here in your browser.**
>
> - Full 3D flight: turn, roll, strafe and dive in any direction through stations, gorges, flooded docks and spinning drum worlds
> - 36 sectors across three episodes, each with its own objectives, music, sky and secrets
> - Bosses at the end of every episode, and more along the way
> - Seven cannon tiers, salvo launchers, seeker missiles and an upgrade bay between sectors
> - Mouse and keyboard, or a phone held sideways
>
> **The full game ($7.99)** is a download that plays in any browser, offline: all 36 sectors, every boss, no ads.
>
> **Keep your progress:** in the demo, press Esc and COPY SAVE CODE, then paste it into the full game's menu.
